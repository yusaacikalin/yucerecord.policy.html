Privacy Policy

Yuce Background Audio Recorder (also called YuCeRecord or Yuce Arka Plan Ses Kaydedici), package com.yuce.loopvoice, is developed by Yuşa Açıkalın.

This policy describes recording, optional cloud features, advertising and diagnostic services in the app. The previous statement that the app uses no advertising, analytics or crash reporting no longer describes the current app.

Recordings and device features

Audio recordings and their associated metadata are stored on your device or in a folder you select. The microphone is used for recording. When you enable optional features and grant the relevant permission, the app can associate location information, call events or contact information with recordings. These features are controlled through the app and Android permission settings. Foreground services and notifications support ongoing recording; alarms, boot handling and battery settings support features you configure.

Audio processing, such as trimming, conversion and waveform generation, runs on the device. The app does not send recording audio to AdMob or Firebase Analytics as advertising or analytics input.

Optional Google Drive and sharing

If you connect a Google account and enable Drive synchronization, recordings selected by your synchronization settings are uploaded to your Google Drive. The app keeps the connected account email and synchronization metadata to operate this feature. Disconnecting Drive stops future authorized synchronization; it does not automatically remove files already uploaded.

If you choose to create a Drive sharing link, the selected recording is uploaded and made accessible to people with that link. You control whether to share it. Local file sharing sends the selected file to the app or recipient you choose. Manage previously uploaded files and sharing permissions in Google Drive. Deleting a local recording may also delete its Drive copy when you have enabled that synchronization option.

Advertising and privacy choices

The free version uses Google AdMob. Depending on your region, choices and available ad serving mode, Google and its advertising partners process identifiers, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), ad and app interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Personalized advertising may use these data to select ads based on interests.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices before ads are requested. You can revisit the available choices using Ad privacy options in the app's settings. Withholding personalization consent does not necessarily remove all advertising; limited or non-personalized ads may be available. Android also provides advertising ID controls. Premium ad removal controls advertising, not the app's other optional services.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Analytics, diagnostics and service operation

The release app uses Firebase Analytics for usage events and Firebase Crashlytics for errors and diagnostic information. These services may process app instance identifiers, device and app information, screen and feature interactions, crash reports and diagnostic logs. The app also reports a daily summary of recording count and total recording duration to Analytics; this summary does not include audio content, recording names or file URIs.

Firebase Messaging, Firebase Installations, Firebase App Check and Google Play services support messages, service identification, integrity checks and app operation. Google Play handles in-app purchases; the app receives purchase status to apply purchased features. Feedback that you explicitly submit is sent through Firebase services together with the information included in the feedback form.

For Google's service-specific practices and retention information, see Firebase privacy and security. Third-party services may process information on servers outside your country.

Retention and deletion

Local recordings remain until you delete them or a retention rule you enable removes them. You can manage recordings in the app or your selected storage folder. Device copies, exported files, shared links and cloud copies must be managed separately according to the options you have enabled. Service providers retain advertising, usage and diagnostic data according to their service settings and published policies. Contact the developer with privacy or deletion requests concerning information submitted directly to the developer; include only the information needed to identify the request.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to the developer, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Changes and contact

This page may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. For privacy questions, contact yusa313@hotmail.com.
